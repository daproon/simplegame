# Five-Minute Teacher Test Script

1. Open the game. Confirm the dragon fills the habitat and the top card says
   **Warm the Moon Egg** at `0 / 20 Stars`.
2. Press **Award Stars**, then **+5 Class goal achieved**. Confirm the Egg
   brightens/shakes and the panel closes automatically.
3. Repeat once. At 10 Stars, confirm the Egg has a visible crack. Open the
   lock icon to see **Start small class choice**.
4. Award two more `+5` rewards. Confirm **Reward Ready** appears without
   starting the hatch.
5. Press **Reward Ready** and choose **Gather warm crystals** or
   **Build a soft nest**.
6. Watch the short sequence or press **Skip to reveal**. Confirm Pip appears,
   15 Dragon Coins are awarded, and the habitat stays changed.
7. Open **Scrapbook** and confirm the Moon Egg page records the choice,
   discovery, date, and Coins.
8. Open **Habitat Shop**, buy the 10-Coin **Star Lantern**, and confirm it
   appears in the habitat.
9. Reload the page. Confirm Pip, the hatched shell, scrapbook page, remaining
   Coins, and Star Lantern are still present.
10. Press `1`, open the lock icon, and press **Undo last award**. Confirm only
    that ordinary award is reversed. Toggle **Quiet mode** and verify progress
    still updates without sound or a reaction.
